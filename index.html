Case study

Matching solar products to B2B requirements, without a human doing the lookup.

Sales engineers stopped being the lookup table and went back to selling.

The situation
A solar manufacturer with a wide, technically dense catalogue. Every B2B enquiry needed a person to interpret the requirements and find the products that actually fit.
What I built
A workflow automation layer that reads the incoming requirement, matches it against the product data, and returns a shortlisted configuration with the constraints it satisfied and the ones it did not.
Why it worked
Because I was closing these deals myself, the system encodes how the product is genuinely sold, not a flowchart drawn by someone who has never been on the call.
